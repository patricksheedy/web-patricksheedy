About Me

Building things for the modern web

I'm a software developer with a passion for creating innovative web applications and sharing knowledge through writing. With years of experience in full-stack development, I enjoy tackling complex problems and building solutions that make a difference.

AI is a core part of how I work. I use AI assistants like Claude and Copilot throughout my development workflow — scaffolding features faster, reviewing code, debugging stubborn issues, and exploring new APIs. I treat AI as a pair programmer: it accelerates the routine work while I stay focused on architecture, correctness, and craft.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open source projects, or writing about the latest trends in software development and AI-assisted tooling.
